# MetricStream

> The World's #1 AI-First Connected GRC Platform — Empowering Organizations with Governance, Risk, and Compliance Solutions.

MetricStream is a global leader in integrated Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) management software. Headquartered in San Jose, California, MetricStream helps organizations proactively manage risk, streamline compliance, strengthen governance, and drive business performance. The platform is trusted by hundreds of leading enterprises across banking, financial services, healthcare, life sciences, energy, manufacturing, and other regulated industries. MetricStream's AI-powered, connected GRC platform enables organizations to break down silos, unify risk and compliance data, and make informed decisions in real time.
